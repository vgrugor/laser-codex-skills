# TikTok Playbook

Use this reference when turning a generated preview or product photo into TikTok content for premium laser-cut plywood products.

## Narrative Spines

| Spine | Use When | Core Tension | Example Positioning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `render-to-reality` | Only a generated preview exists | Can this beautiful concept become a real plywood product? | `Сделаю этот концепт из фанеры и покажу весь путь.` |
| `vote-to-build` | Color, size, personalization, or variant choices are open | The audience helps decide what gets made | `Какую морилку выбрать для первого прототипа?` |
| `premium-proof` | The product may look expensive or needs price justification | Show why it is worth more than a simple cutout | `Вот 4 детали, из-за которых это выглядит дороже.` |
| `gift-occasion` | Product is bought as a gift or home accent | Match product to a person, holiday, room, or emotion | `Идея подарка, которая не выглядит массовой.` |
| `problem-fix` | Prototype or process has visible mistakes | Craft improvement builds trust | `Первая версия почти готова, но одна деталь портит вид.` |
| `final-reveal` | The real product is ready | Pay off the story and invite action | `Помните этот рендер? Вот что получилось из фанеры.` |

Default to `render-to-reality` for generated previews. Combine it with `vote-to-build` when the user needs audience engagement before cutting.

## Phase Plan

| Phase | Product State | Goal | Recommended Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Concept Tease | Generated preview only | Start curiosity without misleading | concept reveal, buyer question, vote on stain/hardware, close-up feature callout |
| 2. Design Prep | CAD/LightBurn not cut yet | Show that the idea is becoming buildable | CAD screen, layer separation, size decision, material choice, risk to solve |
| 3. Cutting | Production starts | Create satisfying proof of craft | laser path, parts coming out, detail close-ups, removing masking, first dry-fit |
| 4. Finishing | Stain/sanding/lacquer | Justify premium feel | sanding before/after, stain tests, color reveal, lacquer sheen, hardware choice |
| 5. Assembly | Product becomes real | Build anticipation toward reveal | glue-up, layer alignment, hardware, personalization, final QA |
| 6. Reveal | Finished product | Convert attention into follows/messages/orders | render-vs-real, wall/table context, gift packaging, order/listing CTA |
| 7. After Launch | Product is sellable | Extend the story | price explanation, variants, customer question replies, batch progress, packaging orders |

## Shot List By Stage

### Preview / Concept

- Screen or printed preview image with clear overlay: `концепт`, `визуализация`, or `буду делать из фанеры`.
- Crop into the most premium detail: layers, engraving, hardware, personalization area, silhouette.
- Hand pointing to the design detail that will be hardest or most satisfying to make.
- Material samples beside the preview: plywood, stains, lacquer, hardware.
- Quick poll frame: `темный/светлый`, `латунь/черный металл`, `с персонализацией/без`.

### CAD / LightBurn

- Cursor tracing the main silhouette or layer boundaries.
- Layer groups separated by color/operation.
- Scale decision beside a ruler or common object.
- Risk frame: tiny bridges, engraved text, thin details, hardware holes.
- Export/nesting preview before cutting.

### Cutting

- Laser starting the first outline.
- Close-up of smoke/exhaust only if clean and visually controlled.
- Parts lifting out of plywood sheet.
- Finger snap or match cut from preview to cut parts.
- Failed or test piece if it teaches something.

### Sanding / Staining / Lacquer

- Before/after sanding edge quality.
- Stain swatch comparison on the same plywood.
- First brush/cloth pass revealing color.
- Dry parts laid in order by layer.
- Matte/satin lacquer close-up in side light.

### Assembly / QA

- Dry-fit from flat pieces to dimensional product.
- Glue dots or clamps only if neat.
- Alignment check from side angle.
- Hardware installation.
- Personalization close-up.
- Final inspection: edge, finish, scale, wall/table context.

### Finished Product / Sales

- 2-second clean hero shot in real use context.
- Hand interaction for scale.
- Detail sweep: engraving, layers, stain contrast, hardware.
- Packaging/gift-ready shot.
- Render-vs-real comparison.
- Variant or personalization examples.

## Video Templates

### 1. Concept Teaser

- Hook: `У меня есть только визуализация, но я хочу сделать это из фанеры. Запускать в работу?`
- Shots: preview image, material samples, one premium detail zoom.
- Text/VO: `Это пока концепт. Если форма зайдет, покажу весь путь: макет, резка, морилка, сборка и финал.`
- CTA: `Напишите "делай", если хотите увидеть результат.`
- Best for: first post from a generated preview.

### 2. Audience Vote

- Hook: `Один и тот же макет, но настроение будет совсем разное. Какой вариант делать?`
- Shots: preview plus 2-3 stain/hardware options.
- Text/VO: `Темная морилка выглядит дороже, светлая дает больше контраста, черный металл делает изделие строже.`
- CTA: `1, 2 или 3?`
- Best for: choosing finish before physical production.

### 3. Buildability Check

- Hook: `Красиво на рендере еще не значит, что это можно нормально вырезать. Проверяю макет.`
- Shots: CAD/LightBurn screen, thin details, layer separation.
- Text/VO: `Ищу слабые места: слишком тонкие перемычки, мелкий текст, отверстия под крепеж, порядок слоев.`
- CTA: `Показывать такие проверки чаще?`
- Best for: turning technical prep into trust.

### 4. First Cut

- Hook: `Момент, когда визуализация впервые становится фанерой.`
- Shots: laser cutting first layer, parts lifting out, preview match cut.
- Text/VO: `Пока это только первая версия. Сейчас станет понятно, держит ли форма премиальный вид в реальном материале.`
- CTA: `Досмотреть до сборки?`
- Best for: production start.

### 5. Stain Transformation

- Hook: `Цвет решает, будет это выглядеть как сувенир или как дорогой декор.`
- Shots: raw plywood, stain pass, dried comparison, preview reference.
- Text/VO: `Проверяю контраст слоев, чтобы рисунок читался без лишней пестроты.`
- CTA: `Темнее или оставить так?`
- Best for: price justification and audience engagement.

### 6. Assembly Suspense

- Hook: `Все детали готовы. Сейчас станет понятно, работает ли идея в объеме.`
- Shots: parts layout, dry-fit, glue-up, side depth, not full final reveal.
- Text/VO: `На этом этапе главное - ровные слои, чистые края и чтобы декор не выглядел как случайный набор деталей.`
- CTA: `Финал показать отдельным роликом?`
- Best for: delaying reveal ethically.

### 7. Render Vs Real Reveal

- Hook: `Помните этот концепт? Вот что получилось из фанеры.`
- Shots: preview image, fast transition, real product hero shot, detail sweep.
- Text/VO: `Я не хотел просто повторить картинку. Важно было сохранить настроение, но сделать вещь реально прочной и аккуратной.`
- CTA: `Хотели бы такой вариант с персонализацией?`
- Best for: first sales-ready reveal.

### 8. Price Proof

- Hook: `Почему такая вещь не должна выглядеть как обычная лазерная заготовка.`
- Shots: layers, stain, hardware, engraving, packaging.
- Text/VO: `Цена держится на дизайне, подготовке макета, ручной обработке, финише, аккуратной сборке и упаковке.`
- CTA: `Напишите "цена", если хотите вариант под заказ.`
- Best for: after reveal, when users ask about cost.

### 9. Comment Reply

- Hook: Use the exact viewer question on screen.
- Shots: answer with one process or product detail.
- Text/VO: Keep it direct: `Да, можно изменить размер / добавить имя / сделать светлее / повесить на стену.`
- CTA: `Задавайте следующий вопрос, отвечу видео.`
- Best for: turning comments into follow-up content.

## Mini Launch Calendar

Use this as a default when no analytics are available. Adjust to the creator's real production speed.

| Day | Status | Post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Preview only | Concept teaser with transparent `это пока визуализация` framing |
| 2 | Preview only | Audience vote on finish, hardware, size, or personalization |
| 3 | CAD/prep | Buildability check or layer separation |
| 4 | Cutting | First cut / first parts from plywood |
| 5 | Finishing | Stain or sanding transformation |
| 6 | Assembly | Dry-fit or assembly suspense without full reveal |
| 7 | Finished or nearly finished | Render-vs-real reveal |
| 8 | Finished | Price proof, use context, or packaging |
| 9+ | After launch | Comment replies, variants, order progress, customer-style POV |

If production takes longer, stretch the same sequence. Do not force fake progress.

## Posting Guidance

- Prefer a consistent short series over one overloaded video.
- Keep most videos 7-25 seconds. Use longer videos only when the process is genuinely satisfying or educational.
- Open with the visual result, the conflict, or the transformation; do not start with a long greeting.
- Put the clearest text overlay in the first second.
- Use one CTA per video: vote, comment, follow for the result, ask for price, or message to order.
- Pin the concept teaser, the render-vs-real reveal, and the ordering/FAQ video when the product is ready.
- Reply to strong comments with new videos, especially questions about price, size, personalization, mounting, color, and durability.
- Use the creator's own analytics for posting time. If analytics do not exist yet, test two repeatable windows for 2-3 weeks instead of relying on universal best-time claims.

## TikTok Readiness Scorecard

Score 1-5.

| Criterion | Score | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Honest preview framing |  | Is it clear what is concept vs real? |
| First-second hook |  | Does the first frame create curiosity? |
| Visible transformation |  | Is there a clear before/after or process step? |
| Craft trust |  | Does the video show skill, care, or material quality? |
| Premium proof |  | Does it justify price visually? |
| Comment potential |  | Is there a simple question viewers can answer? |
| Sales path |  | Is the next action clear without feeling pushy? |

Recommended interpretation:

- 30-35: publish-ready.
- 23-29: revise hook, CTA, or proof detail.
- 16-22: too vague; make the transformation or buyer clearer.
- Under 16: rebuild the video idea.
